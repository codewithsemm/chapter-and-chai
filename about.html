01 / The Story Behind Chapter & Chai

Inspired by the Pink City. Made for the Love of Stories.

Chapter & Chai brings together two simple pleasures: a beautifully brewed cup and a story worth getting lost in. Our space takes its warmth from Jaipur's timeless charm, its unhurried conversations and its welcoming spirit.

We believe a café can be more than somewhere to stop for coffee. It can be your favourite reading corner, a place to meet an old friend, or the beginning of an unexpected conversation.

Come as you are. Stay for a while. Leave with a story.

02 / Our Philosophy

A Good Book. A Warm Cup. A Moment of Your Own.

Some of life's best moments are beautifully simple. The aroma of freshly brewed coffee, the comfort of chai, and a story that makes the world outside fade away.

At Chapter & Chai, we believe slowing down is something to celebrate. Every table is an invitation to pause, discover and enjoy the present.
